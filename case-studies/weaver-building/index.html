Scale on the corridor

A 24-unit mixed-use property that anchors a broader revitalization thesis for Albany Avenue.

The Weaver Building is a corridor-scale project: 24 mixed-use spaces arranged behind one of the portfolio's longest Albany Avenue façades.

The photo record follows the building from an uneven earlier presentation to a brighter, unified storefront line, then inside to residential improvements. The result is not one cosmetic moment, but a sustained program of exterior, common-area, and unit-level work.
