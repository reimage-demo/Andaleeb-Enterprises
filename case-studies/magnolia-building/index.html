Vacancy to mixed-use momentum

A distressed Albany Avenue building repositioned with practical improvements and a tenant-first affordability strategy.

Magnolia is a long-view revitalization story. Photos beginning in 2020 document an aging mixed-use building whose scale and location still made it important to Albany Avenue.

The work progressed in practical phases—storefront openings, façade repairs, safer residential space, and renewed commercial visibility. Later images show a building that once read as tired and fragmented becoming a more coherent, active part of the corridor.
